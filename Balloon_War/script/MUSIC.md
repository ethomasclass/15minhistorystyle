## The three new cues (Suno: Custom, Instrumental ON)

**Exclude Styles (all three):** `vocals, choir, lyrics, humming, EDM, trap, pop, rock, synth lead, dubstep, rap, modern drum kit`

### 1. `b_newsreel`: "the war years" (about 2:00)
Reusable for: any 1930s–1950s video (WWII, the home front, the Great Depression's end, early Cold War), newsreel
montages, D-Day or any landing, a cold open that turns from now to then.
```
Styles: 1940s wartime newsreel orchestral underscore, instrumental, brushed snare and military snare rolls, low brass
chorale, warm strings, solo trumpet, timpani, distant radio-static texture, cinematic but restrained, 72 bpm, minor key
turning major
```
Structure to aim for: 0:00–0:20 a quiet, curious opening (solo muted trumpet over soft strings, a ticking pulse) ·
0:20 first strong hit (timpani + low brass), then a steady march pulse · 0:45–1:20 "dawn": broad, noble strings and
horns, building · 1:20–1:45 thins to strings · a held, dignified final chord near 1:50.
*Pick the take whose first 20 seconds are quiet and curious and whose hit at about 0:20 is clear.*

### 2. `b_caper_40s`: "the trick" (about 2:00)
Reusable for: spies, hoaxes, cons, pranks, propaganda, any "they fooled everyone" story, from the 1920s to the 1960s.
```
Styles: 1940s spy caper underscore, instrumental, sneaky swing, walking upright bass, brushed snare, muted trumpet
with wah, clarinet runs, pizzicato strings, vibraphone, finger snaps, playful and mischievous, 112 bpm, minor key
```
Structure: 0:00–0:10 tiptoe intro (pizzicato + bass) · 0:10–1:00 the sneaky groove (muted trumpet melody) ·
1:00–1:30 a "near miss" break: stop-time hits and a cymbal choke · 1:30–2:00 groove back, ends on a cheeky
button (one muted-trumpet "wah-wah" and a bass note).
*The button matters: it's the gag hit for "Which, to be fair, was the whole point."*

### 3. `b_paper_wind`: "carried by the wind" (about 2:30)
Reusable for: journeys and crossings (migration, voyages, flights, letters, messages), memorials and endings,
anything fragile and beautiful that carries something heavy.
```
Styles: airy cinematic elegy, instrumental, solo felt piano, soft string ensemble, alto flute, harp, celesta, gentle
wind and paper-rustle textures, slow, floating, wonder turning to sorrow turning to warmth, 66 bpm, major key with
minor turns
```
Structure: 0:00–0:50 floating and curious (piano, harp, celesta, wind) · 0:50–1:30 darker: low strings, the melody
falls, sparse · 1:30–1:45 near silence · 1:45–2:30 warm and hopeful: the piano melody returns over full strings,
ending on a long, open, unresolved-then-resolved chord.
*The ending third is the closing-line bed ("ended with paper birds"); the opening is ch04.*
