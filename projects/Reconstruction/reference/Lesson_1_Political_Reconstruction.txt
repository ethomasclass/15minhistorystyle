RECONSTRUCTION

LINCOLN’S PLAN
Lincoln wanted to be generous to attract former southern whites to support Republicans in the future
10% of voters had to take a loyalty oath for a state to rejoin
New constitutions would have to abolish slavery and provide for education of African-Americans
+ recognize 13th Amendment which freed enslvaved. 
Confederate leaders also banned from office holding


LINCOLN’S PLAN
Lincoln also wanted to pardon some Confederate leaders from Treason
He was also open to paying financial damages to former slave owners
He did not push for black voting rights
Radical Republicans in Congress viewed his plan as too weak
They wanted to punish the south harshly to set precedent


RADICAL COMPLAINTS
Radical Republicans complained that it was up to them to come up with terms of readmission
Insisted the planter class be economically destroyed
Wade-Davis Bill= 50% of adults had to take oath and no confederate sympathizers allowed to hold office
Lincoln vetoed the bill but does allow the division of the south into military districts


ANDREW JOHNSON
Lincoln is assassinated and never sees his plan carried out → VP Andrew Johnson becomes POTUS
Johnson was from Tennessee, not well-educated, struggled with alcoholism, and had his sights on ruining the planter class whom he envied all his life
Emancipation was as far as he wanted to go when it came to helping African-Americans


JOHNSON’S PLAN
A majority of southerners would have to take loyalty oath in order to have rights and properties restored
Former confederate officials and rich would have to apply for individual pardons
He informally encouraged, but did not demand states abolish slavery in new constitutions
Far more lenient than Lincoln

FAILURES OF THE PLAN
Several states rejected the 13th Amendment freeing enslaved
Many states passed Black Codes= laws restricting black rights; very similar to slave codes
Intended to keep African-Americans as farm laborers indebted to whites
Several states elect former confederate officials
By 1866, Johnson claimed Reconstruction a success and over

FREEDMEN’S BUREAU
Johnson was stuck with Lincoln’s Freedmen’s Bureau
Freedmen’s Bureau= organization that provided emergency food, clothing, medical care and shelter to newly freed African-Americans
The FB also set up schools across the south for children and adults
helped in legalizing marriages entered into during slavery, and provided transportation to freedpeople who were attempting to reunite with their family 

CONGRESS HAS OTHER IDEAS
A majority of the Congress were Radical Republicans who believed that Reconstruction must completely change the south in the image of the north
There were some moderates who believed African-Americans needed legal protection, but nothing more
Radicals pushed for civil/equal rights including the right to vote
In 1866, Johnson vetoed both an extension of the Freedmen’s Bureau and a Civil Rights Act
Congress able to overturn it
THADDEUS STEVENS

14th AMENDMENT
In 1866, the Radical Republicans draft  the 14 Amendment
14th Amendment= everyone, no matter what race, is a citizen and equal under the law
Rebuttal of Dred Scott Decision
14A was intended to end the era of “black codes” in the south
Johnson publicly criticizes the amendment and tells states to vote against it
It passed because many moderates were disgusted with Johnson by this point and had been drive to the Radical wing
