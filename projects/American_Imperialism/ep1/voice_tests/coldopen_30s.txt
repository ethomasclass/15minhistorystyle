The Queen of Hawaiʻi is a prisoner in her own palace.

And the men who put her here aren't a foreign army. They're mostly Americans. Or the sons of Americans.

In 1776, America told the world something new. A government gets its power from "the consent of the governed." [pause] The people have to say yes.
