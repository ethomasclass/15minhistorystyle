## Missing / weak

- **1820 arrival of the missionaries (engraving):** no period engraving of the arrival was found. Searches covered Commons ("Thaddeus", "first missionaries"), LoC, and the illustration list of Hiram Bingham's *Residence of Twenty-One Years* (1847). The substitute is Bingham's 1847 plate of Kaʻahumanu at Waimea recommending Christianity.
- **1887 constitution document:** no scan found on IA, LoC, Commons or UH eVols. The Hawaiʻi State Archives site loads its records by JavaScript and could not be searched. The substitute is the July 1887 *Wasp* cartoon, which is a racist caricature: use it with care or not at all.
- **Committee of Safety photo:** only a 1280 px Commons copy, scanned from a 1993 book. The Hawaiʻi State Archives original is PP-36-3-001; request it from HSA for a better copy.
- **US flag over the Government Building, Feb. 1, 1893:** no photo of the event was found. Substitutes are the building itself (1898-book plate) and the 1896-book "View of Honolulu" showing the flagstaff on Aliʻiōlani Hale.
- **Blount Report title page:** FRUS 1894 App. II could not be reached (HathiTrust and the UW FRUS site were blocked, and the Commons copy is 743 px). The substitute is the title page of Cleveland's Dec. 18, 1893 message, which transmitted Blount's findings, plus its "act of war" page.
- **Liliʻuokalani's Jan. 17, 1893 statement:** no scan of the signed original was reachable (it is at the Hawaiʻi State Archives). The substitute is the full printed text in Cleveland's 1893 message (page XIX).
- **Dole / Hawaiian Pineapple Co. can label (pre-1930):** none found. LoC's Patent Office trademark deposits have other Hawaiian pineapple brands (Hawaiian Chief 1908, Aloha 1910; the Aloha one is only a word mark and was discarded) but no Hawaiian Pineapple Co. or Dole label. The "Dole" brand name on cans dates from the 1930s, so a pre-1930 "DOLE" label may not exist; any later label would need a copyright/trademark check.
- **1895 imprisonment / trial:** no photo of the room or of the military commission was found. Substitutes from *Hawaii's Story* (1898): the palace front view (its caption marks the corner room where she was held) and the Throne Room, where the caption says the trial took place.

## Rights notes

- `ch06_james_d_dole_1927.jpg`: a 1927 Bain photo. LoC says "No known restrictions", but its public-domain status rests on LoC's Bain assessment, not on age.
