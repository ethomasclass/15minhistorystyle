The Old City of Jerusalem is smaller than a lot of high school campuses. You can walk across it in about 15 minutes.

But packed inside it are some of the holiest places on Earth. The Western Wall, sacred to Jews. The Church of the Holy Sepulchre, where many Christians believe Jesus was buried and rose again. And the Dome of the Rock, sacred to Muslims.

Judaism, Christianity, and Islam are called the Abrahamic religions, because all three trace their story back to Abraham. All three are monotheistic: they believe in one God. And together, they include more than half the people alive today.

So here's the puzzle. These three religions started within about 800 miles of each other. But look at where their followers live now. One is scattered in small communities across the globe. One is centered in Africa and Latin America. And one has its largest population in a country nearly 5,000 miles from where it began.

Same neighborhood. Three totally different maps. Why?
