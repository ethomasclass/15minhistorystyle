# Arsenic Green - archival images, ch02 and ch03 (agent B)

Every file below was downloaded with `tools/find_images.py` / `tools/commons.py`, or by hand from an open-licence source
(Wellcome IIIF, Cooper Hewitt image server, The Met Open Access), and has a credit in `credits_B.json` (keys are relative
to `public/img`; merge into `credits.json`). Sizes are pixels (w x h).

**Real green (the one-time colour exception):** files marked **GREEN** show the actual pigment or a period
hand-coloured green. They're good sources for the `Swatch` colour sample. The rest are engravings or pages that read
fine in black and white.

**Hero / thumbnail:** `ch03/arsenic_waltz_punch_1862.jpg` (Wellcome V0042226, 2974x2358, the largest clean copy that
exists online; it includes the dated *Punch* header and the caption). For masking, use the cleaned crop
`ch03/arsenic_waltz_punch_1862_clean.png`. For the dated document shot, use the full page with verses,
`ch03/arsenic_waltz_punch_1862_page.jpg`.

## Gaps

- **No authenticated life portrait of Scheele.** Every engraving is posthumous or of a disputed sitter; the 1789 medal is
  the nearest period likeness. Tag on screen.
- **The 1778 KVA paper in the original Swedish** (*Kongl. Vetenskaps Academiens Handlingar* 1778) isn't on IA or Commons.
  The English 1786 translation (`scheele_new_green_colour_1778_essays1786_p253.jpg`) stands in. BHL may have it; it needs an API key.
- **Schweinfurt c.1814:** only a 1648 Merian view and a small 1847 steel engraving. Optional beat anyway.
- **BMJ via PMC (PMC2324313):** PMC and Europe PMC block automated PDF download (captcha/403). The same page came from the
  IA microfilm of BMJ 1862 vol. 1 instead; it's clean, readable and dated.
- **Arsenic Waltz at more than 2974 px:** no larger copy exists. Wellcome holds one copy; Commons mirrors it; the IA
  *Punch* vol. 42 page is a microfilm scan (cartoon about 2400 px wide); HathiTrust copies are low-res for downloads. If the
  thumbnail needs more, upscale the Wellcome file.
- **Green toys and candy wrappers as objects:** no open-licence photos of arsenic-green toys or sweet wrappers were found
  (Met/Commons/LOC; the LOC search API returned 503 throughout). Covered by the Lozenge-Maker; the Blodgett parlour shows
  1864 toys; the paper-doll nursery is pink.
- **Green artificial-flower wreaths with arsenic confirmed** (V&A, Bata): not free. The Met 1863 headdress (green leaves)
  and the wreaths in the fashion plates and the Arsenic Waltz stand in.
- **Surviving gowns with arsenic confirmed** (Toronto Met. Univ., Bata boots): rights-restricted. The Met 1868 dress is
  "probably", not tested.
- **Tarlatan specifically:** no open-licence photo of a green tarlatan ball dress. The c.1865 French sheer evening dress
  (met:84436) is the nearest.
- **ILN 1860s ballroom:** not found on Commons; the 1862 *Punch* "The Ball" and the two 1860 New York ball prints cover it.
  ILN weekly issues are on IA (`sim_illustrated-london-news_1862-*`) if a British ballroom engraving is wanted later.
- **Green book cloth with arsenic confirmed** (Winterthur/Delaware "Poison Book Project"): their photos' licence is
  unverified. The 1858 BL emerald cloth is a CC0 stand-in.
