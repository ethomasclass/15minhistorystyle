# Arsenic Green - archival images, agent A (ch01, ch06, ch08, maps)

The American thread. Downloaded with `tools/find_images.py` / `tools/commons.py` (credit in `credits_A.json`). The 1873 LOC map was fetched by hand from the LOC image server, and its credit entry was written by hand. Files named `*_crop*` (and the joined `mac_view_durant_1880_spread.jpg`) were cut from the full page beside them. Their credit entry copies the parent's credit and adds the crop box. Sizes are pixels (w x h).

**Credit lines.** Every Kedzie item (`*kedzie*` except the Beal portrait) needs the credit "Courtesy of the National Library of Medicine". The scans come from the NLM copy, mirrored on IA as `0234555.nlm.nih.gov`. The Draper report is an 1872 Massachusetts state document (Boston Library Consortium scan on IA). The map is credited to the Library of Congress, Geography and Map Division.

**Kedzie leaf numbers.** `nNNN` is the IA scan leaf. The specimen leaves sit on even leaves from n18 to n184, with a tissue guard between each pair, so specimen number ≈ (n − 16) / 2. Leaf n18 is the first wallpaper after the essay. That makes it Kedzie's "first specimen leaf", the paper from the Spring Port house, though this is inferred from its position in the book.

## Gaps

- **Children's paint box (object):** no clearly public-domain image of an 1870s paint box was found. The Met search endpoint is broken, AIC/CMA have nothing period-American, and Smithsonian's NMAH "Outfit for watercolor painting" has no CC0 flag. Use Draper's p.28/p.29 text, or one swatch row.
- **Boston confectioner's shop window:** no period image. Use the Washington & Franklin Sts. photo plus the p.31 text.
- **Michigan farmhouse interior / bedroom (Emma):** no suitable PD engraving. Keep the planned Gemini painting. The Springport atlas exteriors can stand in.
- **Portraits:** the PD Draper (1923 book) and Kedzie (1915 book) portraits both show them old, decades after 1872/74. Kedzie's is low-res (850 px wide). No younger PD portrait was found, and the MSU Kedzie portrait is not usable (rights).
- **Lansing in 1874 exactly:** the 1866 bird's-eye view and the 1880 lithographs bracket the date. The new capitol was unfinished in 1874.
- **Springport village street view:** no period view found. The 1874 township map and farm lithographs are the closest. Sanborn maps (1892+, Commons) exist if needed.
- **Map alternates:** the Keith Johnston 1879 North Atlantic and the H. H. Lloyd 1874 world Mercator are David Rumsey scans (CC BY-NC-SA on IA), so they were not used.
