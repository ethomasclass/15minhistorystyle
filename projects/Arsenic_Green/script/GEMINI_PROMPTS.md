# Dressed to Kill: Gemini painting prompts

Three paintings for moments with no surviving image. Everything else in the video is archival.

**How to run them**
- Model: Gemini image generation, at its largest size. Aspect **16:9**.
- Paste the **whole** prompt, including the style paragraph at the end. The style paragraph is identical in all three, so the paintings read as one series.
- Regenerate rather than fix: anything with text, letters or signs, extra fingers, modern objects, or wrong period details.
- Upload the PNGs with the file names below. If you skip one, the scene still works (an engraving takes its place).
- On screen they're tagged `Illustration · <what it shows>` and shown in black and white, except that the green wallpaper may keep its colour (the video's one-time arsenic-green exception).
