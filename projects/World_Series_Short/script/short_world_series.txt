It's called the World Series. Teams from outside the U.S. have won it exactly twice. Both times, it was Toronto.

So why "World"?

You've probably heard it's named after a newspaper, the New York World. Great story. *Not true.* That paper never sponsored it, and the earliest anyone's found that claim in print is 1991.

The real answer is older, and a lot more confident.

1884. The champions of two rival leagues play each other, and the papers call it the "World's Championship Series." Not because the world was invited. Because as far as they were concerned, the best baseball on Earth was being played right here.

The name stuck. "World's Championship Series" became the "World's Series." Then just: the World Series.

So it's not named after a newspaper. It's named after a *brag*. And it's been bragging for over 140 years.
