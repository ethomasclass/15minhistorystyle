## Fact-check flags

1. **"Teams from outside the U.S. have won it exactly twice. Both times, it was Toronto."** The Toronto Blue Jays won in 1992 and 1993, the only non-U.S. champions. The "2 of 121" on the map assumes 121 Series before 2026 (1903–2025, with none in 1904 or 1994). **Re-check before posting.** If Toronto is in or wins the 2026 Series, change the hook to "won it twice, maybe three times."
2. **The New York World myth.** Snopes: the paper "never had anything to do with the World Series… other than being one of the many newspapers to report the results"; the earliest citation found so far is 1991. The Hall of Fame says there's no evidence of sponsorship.
3. **1884.** The National League champion Providence Grays played the American Association's New York Metropolitans. The series was promoted as the "World's Championship Series," and newspapers called the Grays "World Champions."
4. **"As far as they were concerned, the best baseball on Earth was being played right here."** This is a paraphrase of the common explanation that the label meant the best professional teams were American. It's phrased as their attitude, not a quote.
5. **"World's Series" to "World Series."** The longer form was common in the early 1900s and shortened over time. The script gives no exact dates.
6. **"Over 140 years."** 1884 to 2026 is 142 years.
