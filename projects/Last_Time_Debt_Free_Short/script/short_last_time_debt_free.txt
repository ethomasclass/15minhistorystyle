When was the last time America didn't owe anybody a single dollar?

January 1835. The national debt hit *zero*. The only time in U.S. history.

President Andrew Jackson hated debt. Land sales were booming, money poured in, and he paid off every cent. Washington threw a party.

It lasted about two years.

That land boom was a bubble. People borrowed to buy land they'd never seen, and banks printed paper money to keep up. Then the government said: pay for land in gold or silver.

In 1837, the banks ran short. People lined up to pull out their savings. Hundreds of banks failed. It was called the Panic of 1837, and the depression after it lasted about six years. Historians still argue over exactly what caused it.

And the government? It had to start borrowing again.

So, the last time America was debt-free? 1835. For about two years.
