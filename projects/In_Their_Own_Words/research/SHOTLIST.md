# In Their Own Words: archival shot list

Save to `public/img/chNN/` with descriptive names. Get an alternate for each must-have. B&W engravings, lithographs, photographs, documents and maps. Real people need a real portrait (photo or engraving).

## ch01 Cold Open
- MUST: Alexander H. Stephens portrait (Brady studio photo or engraving; a clean, frontal one masks best)
- MUST: Title page or frontispiece of *A Constitutional View of the Late War Between the States* (1868), Internet Archive scan
- Savannah in 1861 (view of the city or the Athenaeum where he spoke, if any)
- A period newspaper column reporting the Cornerstone Speech (Savannah Republican, March 1861) if a scan exists

## ch02 One Election
- MUST: Lincoln 1860 campaign portrait (e.g., Brady Cooper Union photo Feb 1860) or 1860 campaign poster/banner
- 1860 election map or results broadside
- MUST: "The Union is Dissolved!" Charleston Mercury extra broadside, December 20, 1860
- Secession convention / Secession Hall, Charleston
- Jefferson Davis portrait; his inauguration at Montgomery, February 1861 (photo exists)

## ch03 In Their Own Words
- MUST: Georgia's declaration of causes (scan of the document or its printed journal page)
- Mississippi declaration of causes (printed version)
- MUST: South Carolina "Declaration of the Immediate Causes" printed pamphlet
- Stephens again (alternate portrait)
- Lost Cause: a Confederate monument dedication photo (pre-1929), a UCV (United Confederate Veterans) reunion photo; Jefferson Davis's *Rise and Fall of the Confederate Government* (1881) title page

## ch04 Which Georgians?
- MUST: Milledgeville statehouse (Georgia's capitol in 1861) engraving or photo
- Georgia secession ordinance (scan)
- 1860 census page or a "Map showing the distribution of the slave population of the southern states" (1861 U.S. Coast Survey map) — MUST, it's perfect for "44 percent"

## ch05 Fort Sumter
- MUST: Bombardment of Fort Sumter engraving/lithograph (Currier & Ives or Harper's)
- Fort Sumter interior after surrender, April 1861 (photos exist)
- Major Robert Anderson portrait
- Lincoln's call for 75,000 volunteers (proclamation broadside or newspaper)
- Lincoln's blockade proclamation, April 19, 1861
- Border states: a period map or cartoon about Kentucky/Maryland neutrality

## ch06 Sizing Up the Sides
- MUST: Scott's Great Snake (J.B. Elliott, 1861) Anaconda Plan cartoon map
- Winfield Scott portrait
- Robert E. Lee portrait (early war, or 1860s Brady)
- A Northern factory or arsenal (Springfield Armory, Tredegar Iron Works in Richmond for contrast)
- Railroads: an 1861 railroad map of the U.S. (LoC has "Lloyd's American railroad map" 1861)
- King Cotton: cotton bales on a wharf or a "King Cotton" cartoon

## ch07 A Picnic at Bull Run
- MUST: Union retreat from Bull Run engraving (Harper's Weekly, August 1861) showing civilians/carriages
- Battle of Bull Run lithograph
- Map of the battlefield (period)
- "Forward to Richmond" newspaper header (New York Tribune) if available
- Spectators / Centreville hill sketch (Alfred Waud drawings at LoC)

## ch08 What They Walked Into
- MUST: Union soldiers in camp (photo); Confederate soldiers (photo of Confederate camp or prisoners)
- MUST: Minié ball / rifled musket (Springfield 1861 photo; engraving of a Minié ball from a period manual)
- A field hospital photo (e.g., Savage's Station 1862) or amputation kit; field surgery engraving
- Camp life: drill, letter writing, cooking
- MUST: Andersonville prison photo (August 1864, A.J. Riddle photos at LoC)
- A soldier's letter (any period letter scan)
