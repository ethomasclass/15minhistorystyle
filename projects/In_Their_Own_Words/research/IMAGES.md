# In Their Own Words: archival images

All files are in `public/img/chNN/`. Credits are in `public/img/credits.json` (98 entries, one per file, all present). Source ids: `loc:` Library of Congress, `iabook:<book>:<scan page>` Internet Archive pre-1930 scan, `commons:` Wikimedia Commons. Contact sheets: `out/contact_ch01.jpg` to `out/contact_ch09.jpg`.

Notes:
- The `hw_` (Harper's Weekly) and `iln_` (Illustrated London News) files are full newspaper pages from microfilm, so they are grey and need cropping to the engraving. The pages are large (about 4100 x 5700), so crops stay sharp.
- Two files were rotated upright after download (noted in credits.json): `hw_charge_69th_ny...` and `hw_retreat_of_our_troops...`.
- Commons downloads are capped at 1920 px by the tool (Anaconda map, slave population map).
