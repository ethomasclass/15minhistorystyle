# In Their Own Words (Civil War series, Video 1 of 3)

A 15 Minute History video, **about 9:45**, 9 chapters, for general viewers and for students (Unit 4, Lesson 5 and part of Lesson 6:
secession, Fort Sumter, sizing up the sides, Bull Run, soldiers' lives).
