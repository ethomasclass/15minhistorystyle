Two rivers. The Nueces, up here. And the Rio Grande, about 150 miles south. Or, the Noo-ay-sis.

The Tejanos, Mexican Texans. A Tejano captain. Stephen F. Austin, an empresario.

Mexico had joined Texas to a bigger state, Coahuila. Or, Ko-ah-wee-lah.

General Antonio López de Santa Anna takes charge of Mexico.

October 1835. Gonzales, Texas. "Come and take it."

José Antonio Navarro of San Antonio. A Tejano captain, Juan Seguín. Or, Juan Seh-geen.

Three weeks later, at Goliad. April 21st, 1836. San Jacinto. Or, San Juh-sin-toe.

The Mexican prisoners call out, "El Presidente!"

Across the river from the Mexican town of Matamoros. The Californios, Mexican Californians.

Scott lands at Veracruz. The castle of Chapultepec. The Niños Héroes, the boy heroes. Or, the Neen-yos Air-oh-es.

They're San Patricios: mostly Irish Catholic immigrants. Two of them meet at Appomattox, Virginia.

The Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo. Or, Gwah-dah-loo-pay Ee-dahl-go. Article 8 says their property will be "inviolably respected."

The Wilmot Proviso. It passes the House, 83 to 64. The House votes, 85 to 81.

About 530,000 square miles. Up to 25 million dollars. The United States pays 15 million dollars.

About 1,700 Americans were killed in battle. More than 11,500 died of disease. On May 13th, 1846, Congress declares war.
