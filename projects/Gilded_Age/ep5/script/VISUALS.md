# Episode 5 visuals plan ("Fighting Back", script draft 9, voiced Nov 18: 15:14)

Rules as always: Kellys coral `#FF6F61` (ink outlines); Rabinowitzes wheat gold `#E2B864`; everyone else ink; teal explains; orange
torn strips and handwriting; money only a small gold glint. Real people never get faces (Carnegie, Frick, Gompers, Coxey, Bryan,
McKinley, Garfield: archival portraits, or faceless silhouettes). Violence off-screen (glow, smoke, flashes); nobody shown hurt.
No figure kneels or crouches near another's lap. Text at least 2.5 s, never cut off.

## The three doors (signature graphic, `lookdev/ep5_doors.js`)
Three tall doors drawn in the notebook style, labelled on torn tape: AT WORK (union emblem: clasped hands), ON THE FARM (wheat sheaf),
AT THE BALLOT BOX (ballot box). Teal light behind each door = hope.
- ch 1 end: the three doors introduced (each lights up on "fought at work / teamed up on the farm / fought at the ballot box").
- ch 5 end ("The first door had closed"): door 1, light pouring through, slams shut; dust; orange "FELL SHORT" stamp.
- ch 6 end ("The second door had closed"): door 2 shuts the same way.
- ch 9 end ("The third door had closed"): door 3 shuts.
- ch 10: camera walks along the three closed doors ("fought back, fell short" x3, a stamp each); then light leaks under the doors and
  the ideas float out as glowing cards (EIGHT-HOUR DAY, RIGHT TO ORGANIZE, INCOME TAX, SENATORS ELECTED BY VOTERS: "STILL WANTED");
  final beat: far down the corridor a fourth door labelled PROGRESSIVE ERA cracks open, teal light (the handoff line).

## Explainers (teal, `lookdev/ep5_explain.js`)
- Owners' weapons vs workers' weapons (ch 3): two columns that fill: UNION, COLLECTIVE BARGAINING, STRIKE vs BLACKLIST, LOCKOUT, STRIKEBREAKERS, COURTS AND SOLDIERS.
- Two kinds of unions (ch 4): Knights (everyone, big goals, arbitration) vs AFL (skilled, bread and butter, collective bargaining); the Knights' side cracks after Haymarket.
- Who's in charge (ch 5): ANDREW CARNEGIE (owner) above HENRY CLAY FRICK (runs it) above THE HOMESTEAD MILL.
- Owen's three problems (ch 6): debt, railroads, falling prices (icons).
- Owen's loan (ch 7): $1,000 debt; a stack of 1,000 bushels at $1, then 2,000 bushels at 50 cents ("same debt, twice the work").
- Inflation (ch 7): greenbacks pour in, the same goods, price tags climb.
- Gold standard (ch 7): a vault of gold; each dollar chained to a bar.
- The bread town (ch 7): 100 dollars, 100 loaves at $1; 200 loaves, same 100 dollars, price tags drop to 50 cents ("deflation").
- Free silver (ch 7): silver coins join the gold, wheat price climbs back toward $1.
- Spoils system (ch 8): a line of job seekers at a door; the civil service test card.
- Omaha Platform (ch 8): the demands as a checklist (they reappear in ch 10 as the floating ideas).
- 1896 map (ch 9): states fill in, farm states for Bryan, cities and the Northeast for McKinley.
