# YouTube playlist text: The Gilded Age series

## Title

The Gilded Age (1870 to 1900): Full Series for US History

## Description

America from about 1870 to 1900 looked like solid gold: steel mills, railroads, electric light and mansions on Fifth Avenue. Underneath, millions of people worked long hours for low pay in crowded cities. Mark Twain had a name for it: the Gilded Age. Gold on the outside, cheap metal underneath.

This series follows one fictional working family, the Kellys of Brooklyn, through the real events of the era, alongside the tycoons, immigrants, bosses, workers and farmers who shaped it. Each episode runs about 10 to 15 minutes and builds on the last.

1. Industrialization and the Tycoons: Carnegie, Rockefeller, steel, oil and monopolies
2. Wealth and Society: the mansions, the maids, Social Darwinism and the Gospel of Wealth
3. Immigration: Castle Garden, Ellis Island, tenements, name changes and nativism
4. Cities and Political Machines (coming soon)
5. Labor and Unions (coming soon)
6. Farmers and the Populists (coming soon)
Then: a short Progressive Era unit on how Americans responded.

Made for middle and high school US History classrooms (and anyone curious). Watch in order: each part picks up where the last one left off.
