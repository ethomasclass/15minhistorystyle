# Suno prompts: Gilded Age, Episode 3

Same settings, exclude list and palette as Episodes 1 and 2 (`../../ep1/script/SUNO_PROMPTS.md`): Custom mode, Instrumental OFF, paste
the tags box into Lyrics, 2 to 4 takes, keep the take whose first 30 to 40 s is right, download MP3 with the exact name.
Gilt = parlor piano, music box, celesta. Underneath = low cello, muted brass. The Kellys = low whistle, fiddle, harp, concertina.
New for this episode, the Rabinowitzes = klezmer clarinet, violin, accordion (modal, not jokey, never "comedy klezmer").
The script is not written yet: lengths are generous, and placement is confirmed after voicing.

Reused: `ga_gilded_theme` (Episode 1) carries the ending and the end screen.
