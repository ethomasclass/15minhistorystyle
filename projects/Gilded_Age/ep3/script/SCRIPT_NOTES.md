Narration rules for the chart:
- **The Kellys break it.** They are "old" immigrants (Ireland, about 1870) and Catholic. Say so: Irish Catholics were the

- **Row 4 is a perception, not a fact.** Write it as "seen as similar" / "seen as different," and say who was doing the seeing
  (native-born Protestant Americans).
- **1880 is a rough line.** In 1884 new immigrants were still a minority of arrivals (about 11,000 of 500,000+ were Jewish); the
  wave peaks in the 1890s to 1900s. Pair the chart with the decade bar chart already planned.

## The enclave (user request: immerse viewers)

Beat 5 becomes a walk through the neighborhood, about 60 to 75 s, before the sweatshop. Point of view: Rivke, first morning,
following her father. Sensory and specific:
- Street level: pushcarts (Hester Street market), herring barrels, used clothes, the noise; Yiddish and Hebrew letters on signs.
- Tenement: five or six floors, a dark stair, one sink on the hall, boarders sleeping in the front room to help pay rent.
- Institutions that made it an enclave: the little shul over a store, the kosher butcher, the bathhouse, the landsmanshaft hall
  where people from their own town pay dues for a doctor, a sick fund and a burial plot.
- Why the enclave mattered: language, jobs found through people from home, credit, food, worship, and protection from people
  like the runner. It is the answer to "who helped." Also the cost: crowding (Riis, 330,000 per square mile, "it is said"),
  disease, and staying apart from the rest of the city.
- Parallel: the Irish built the same thing a generation earlier (parish, saloon, Irish Emigrant Society bank). Ma Kelly's
  neighborhood is an enclave too.

Visuals: Riis photos (about 1889), Hester Street 1897 (dated on screen), contact sheets in `research/sheets/`. Verify each
institution's presence on the Lower East Side by 1884 before voicing.

### Where the family lives: decision

The research flagged Russian Jews in Williamsburg in 1884 as early and rare (the big wave came after the 1903 bridge). An
enclave walk needs the Lower East Side. **Decision: the Rabinowitzes live on the Lower East Side** (Yankel has boarded there
since 1882). The stoop scene with Ma Kelly is dropped; the Kelly link moves to a single crossing in the script (to be drafted)
and to the Irish-enclave parallel. Ep 4 setting notes stand.
