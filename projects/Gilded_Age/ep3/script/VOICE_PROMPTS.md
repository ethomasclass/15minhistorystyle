# Episode 3 voice design prompts (ElevenLabs Voice Design)

Paste the description into "Describe your voice" and the preview text into the text box. Generate 3, pick one, save with the name

## Sore Rabinowitz (save as "GA Sore")
Description: A warm, low-pitched woman in her mid-thirties with a soft Eastern European Yiddish accent, as if English is her second
language and she speaks it carefully. Tired from a long journey but steady and protective, a mother holding things together.
Gentle, unhurried, slightly husky. Natural, not theatrical. Clean studio recording, close microphone, no background noise.
Preview text: My husband. Yankel Rabinowitz. He said he would be here, by the gate, when the ship came in. Until we see your father,
we keep walking. Hold your brother's hand, and don't let go.

## Rivke Rabinowitz (save as "GA Rivke")
Description: A young woman in her early twenties with a light, bright, slightly high voice that reads younger than her age. Quick
and curious, sharp-eared, a faint Eastern European Yiddish accent that is fading as she learns English fast. Earnest, a little
bold, polite when translating for her mother. Clean studio recording, close microphone, no background noise.
Preview text: Mama, stop. Look, the boats are that way, not this way. My mother asks: where do you buy a ticket for someone to come
from the old country? It's for her sister.

## Yankel Rabinowitz (save as "GA Yankel")
Description: A gentle, tired man in his late thirties with a medium-low voice and an Eastern European Yiddish accent. A garment
presser who works long hours; kind, worn, quietly relieved. Slightly rough texture, warm. Natural and understated. Clean studio
recording, close microphone, no background noise.
Preview text: Sore! Sore, over here! I'm sorry, the shop kept us late. Come, give me the trunk. It's not far. Two streets, and up
the stairs. You'll see. It's small, but it's ours.

## The Runner (save as "GA Runner")
Description: A smooth, friendly man around forty with a medium voice and an Eastern European Yiddish accent. Charming and quick,
smiling as he talks, a salesman's warmth that is a little too eager. Confident, persuasive, never menacing out loud. Clean studio
recording, close microphone, no background noise.
Preview text: Welcome, welcome! Who are you waiting for? Yankel! Of course, of course. He sent me. Come, come, I'll take the trunk.
It's late, you're tired, I know a good place. Very cheap. Follow me.
