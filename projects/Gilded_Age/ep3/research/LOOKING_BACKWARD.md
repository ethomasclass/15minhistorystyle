**Handling:** CARICATURE. The shadows (and the newcomer) are drawn as ethnic types; the peddler shadow has a hooked nose, a common antisemitic convention of the time. The read keeps to the idea (past selves behind present selves), uses rings and lines rather than lingering on faces, and the narration frames it.
