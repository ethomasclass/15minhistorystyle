# Suno prompts: Gilded Age, Episode 1 (and the series)

Copy each **Style** box and its **Lyrics** box into Suno. File names start with `ga_`. Times are from the voiced narration
(Nov 7, 12:32); chapter breaks add a few seconds in the edit. Minimum lengths are the span each cue has to cover plus about 10 s.

**The sound of this series:** the music works like the palette.
- **The gilt:** parlor piano, music box and celesta: polished, glittering, a little too pretty.
- **What's underneath:** low cello and muted brass.
- **The machines:** col legno strings, anvil and timpani.
- **The Kellys:** Irish low whistle, fiddle, harp and concertina, kept for the family only (the way coral is only for the Kellys).
- No jokes in the music beyond a light button now and then; the narration carries the wit. Restrained throughout: you're teaching, not trailering.

## Settings (same for every cue)

- **Custom** mode, **Instrumental OFF**, and paste only the tags box into Lyrics. Every line is in [brackets] and it starts with
  [Instrumental], so there are no words to sing. If a take hums or sings, throw it out.
- **Exclude styles** (every cue):

```
vocals, choir, lyrics, humming, singing, EDM, trap, pop, rock, synth, dubstep, rap, modern drum kit, electric guitar, horror, eerie, jump scare, drone, string tremolo, epic trailer, bagpipes, jig, reel, pub band
```

- Make **2 to 4 takes**; keep the one whose **first 30 to 40 seconds** already sound right.
- **Length:** at least the minimum shown; longer is fine (I trim and fade). If a take is short, **Extend** from its last good section.
- Suno doesn't change mood well inside one song. The mood turns are written into the section tags. If a take ignores them, keep
  the take that's right for the *start* and tell me; I can join two takes.
- **Download as MP3**, name it exactly as shown, and send it back. I'll measure, level and place each one.
