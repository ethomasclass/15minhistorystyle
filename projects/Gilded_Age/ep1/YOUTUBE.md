## Description

In 1883, a few men were getting richer than kings, and a 19-year-old in a Brooklyn sugar refinery was working ten-hour shifts. How did America get so rich, so fast... and who ended up with the money?

Part 1 of The Gilded Age follows two boys who started out poor: Andrew Carnegie, the weaver's son who came to own every step of making steel, and John D. Rockefeller, the con man's son who bought up his rivals and built Standard Oil. Along the way: the Bessemer process, Edison's real invention, a sugar factory that filtered sugar through animal bones, the railroads that invented time zones, and the bad wire hidden inside the Brooklyn Bridge.

The Kelly family is fictional. Everything that happens to them really happened to people like them.
