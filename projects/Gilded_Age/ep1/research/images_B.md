# Archival images, batch B: ch03 The Machines, ch04 The Refinery, ch05 The Weaver's Son

Found and downloaded with `find_images.py` (and its `commons.py` helper, used at 960 or 1280 px for originals too small for the 1920 px default). Credits are recorded in `public/img/credits_B.json`, with all 48 files present.

**Credits note for whoever merges.** 17 of my entries (the four `ch03/bessemer_*` files, all the `iabook:` downloads, and two files I later deleted: `ch04/refinery_interior_1916.jpg` and `ch04/rhinelander_sugar_house_check.jpg`) were also written into **`credits_A.json`**, probably because two agents were writing credit files at the same moment. I copied them into `credits_B.json` and did not touch `credits_A.json`. When merging, drop the two entries for deleted files and any duplicates.

**Size notes.** Commons downloads are capped at 1920 px wide by the tool. The Harper's Weekly copy negatives (Bessemer) are 3200+ px originals; if a crop needs more pixels, fetch the original from the Commons page. LOC stereographs are 3000 px wide for the whole card, so each half is about 1400 px.

Show "Dec 1868" at the top, with a little ink blotting and some crossings-out. Label it "a re-creation".
2. **Edison's electric meter, clean.** Latimer's Fig. 25 probably shows it, but needs a caption check. **Prop:** an 1882 Edison chemical meter: a small varnished wooden box (about 10 x 8 inches) with a hinged front, holding two glass jars, each with a pair of zinc plates hanging in zinc-sulphate solution, a coiled copper shunt and a small carbon-filament lamp at the bottom as a heater. The meter man weighed the plates monthly to compute the bill.
