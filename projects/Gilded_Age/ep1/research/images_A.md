## Gaps (no usable archival image found; generated cut-out prop or drawn animation)

1. **"Some of the steel wire... had already failed its safety test." / "Each of its four main cables is more than five thousand steel wires"**
   (the x-ray). No period cross-section of a Brooklyn Bridge cable was found. *Drawn animation:* a circle 15¾ inches across, made of
   **19 strands** (one centre strand, a ring of 6, an outer ring of 12), each strand a hexagonal bundle of about 278 parallel wires,
   5,434 wires in all, wrapped in an outer spiral of soft galvanized wire. Teal line on paper; a few wires in one strand turn coral
   for "failed". (Verify the strand and wire counts against `../RESEARCH.md` before drawing exact numbers on screen.)
2. **"Every load of wire had to pass a strength test at the factory..."** No image of the 1870s wire-testing machine. *Cut-out
   prop:* an 1870s horizontal tensile-testing machine: a long cast-iron bed on short legs, a screw or hydraulic ram at one end, a
   beam-scale with sliding poise weight at the other, a 16-inch sample of bright steel wire clamped between two jaws. Plus **a coil
   ("ring") of galvanized steel wire**, about 2 feet across and tied with wire in three places, with a cardboard shipping tag.
3. **"A wagon of good wire passed the test and got its certificate."** *Cut-out props:* (a) an 1870s heavy two-horse box freight
   wagon (dray), iron-tyred wooden wheels, low plank sides, stacked with wire coils; (b) **an inspection certificate**: a small
   printed paper slip, copperplate heading "New York and Brooklyn Bridge, Inspector's Office", handwritten lot number, weight,
