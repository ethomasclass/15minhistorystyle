The richest man in American history had a father who charged him interest.

John D. Rockefeller's father sold homemade medicine he said could cure almost anything. Even cancer. It couldn't. He vanished for months at a time, and, it turned out, had a second wife under a fake name.

So John's mother ran the farm, and counted every penny. John learned to count them too.

As a boy, he lent 50 dollars of his turkey money to a neighbor, and got it back with interest. At 16, he wrote down every penny he spent in a little red notebook.

At 19, he borrowed 1,000 dollars from his father to start a business. *His father charged him interest.*

He paid it back. Then he built Standard Oil, and controlled about 90 percent of America's oil.

How he did it is on Chunk History.
