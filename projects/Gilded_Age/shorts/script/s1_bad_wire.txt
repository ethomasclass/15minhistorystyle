Some of the wire holding up the Brooklyn Bridge... *failed its safety test*. They used it anyway.

Every load of wire had to pass a strength test before it went to the bridge. The supplier found a way around it.

A wagon of good wire passed the test and got its certificate. On the way to the bridge, it stopped at another building. The good wire came off. Wire that had *failed* went on. Same wagon. Same certificate. And the good wire went back to be tested. Again.

They caught on when someone noticed the pile of rejected wire... never got any bigger.

By then, tons of bad wire were already in the cables. So the engineers added 150 extra wires to each one. The bridge had been built six times stronger than it needed to be.

It held. People still walk across it every day.
