**Title:** The Brooklyn Bridge Was Built With Bad Wire

**Description:**
A supplier swapped tested wire for wire that had failed, using the same wagon and the same certificate.
Tons of it ended up inside the Brooklyn Bridge's cables. Here's why it still stands.

**Title:** Rockefeller's Father Charged Him Interest

**Description:**
His father sold fake cures and kept a second wife under a fake name. His mother counted every penny.

## s3_vanderbilt_ball.mp4

**Title:** They Spent $9 Million on One Party (1883)

Alternates: The Craziest Party of the Gilded Age / She Came to the Party Dressed as Electricity

**Description:**
In 1883, Alva Vanderbilt threw a costume ball that reportedly cost about $250,000, around $9 million today. Guests
came as cats, hornets and electricity. Here's why she did it, and what it looked like from the kitchen.

## Covers

Covers are in `../thumbnails/`: `s1_cover.jpg` (s1_bad_wire) and `s2_cover.jpg` (s2_rockefeller), 1080x1920; options and
comparison sheets (`s1_sheet.jpg`, `s2_sheet.jpg`) beside them, built by `thumbnails/src/` (`node render.mjs ../options`).
Each cover is embedded in its MP4 as cover art (attached_pic; the video timing is unchanged). YouTube Shorts do not use
that embedded image: pick the cover in the YouTube app from a frame of the video (or upload the JPG where the app offers it).
