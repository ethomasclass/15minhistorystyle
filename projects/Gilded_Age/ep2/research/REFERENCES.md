# Episode 2: visual references for the drawn sets

Look-development references only. All files passed the find_images.py public-domain checks; credits in
`public/img/credits_ref.json`. Files live in `public/img/ref/<scene>/`; contact sheets in `research/ref_sheets/<scene>.jpg`.
"LoC" = Library of Congress Prints and Photographs ("no known restrictions"). Hunt drawings are from the Richard Morris Hunt
collection at LoC (the house as designed and built, 1879 to 1882).
