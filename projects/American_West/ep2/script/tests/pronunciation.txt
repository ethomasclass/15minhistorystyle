Russell called it Waiting for a Chinook. A chinook is a warm wind that melts the snow.

The Goodnight-Loving Trail ran west across Texas, then north, up the Pecos River into New Mexico. The first big one was the Chisholm Trail, up through Indian Territory to Abilene.

The Army was holding about eight thousand Diné, or Navajo, people, along with hundreds of Mescalero Apache. Troops led by Kit Carson burned their fields. The camp was called Bosque Redondo.

One of their riders was a young Black man named Bose Ikard. Across the Rio Grande, to Mexico, where slavery was illegal.

And then there were the vaqueros, Mexican cowboys. They taught the trade, and you can still hear it in the words. Lariat, from la reata. Chaps, from chaparreras. Ranch, from rancho. Rodeo, from rodeo. And bronco, from bronco. It means rough.

A farmer in DeKalb, Illinois, named Joseph Glidden got a patent. The Lakota said no. By 1876, Deadwood had thousands of people. They bought a claim called the Homestake.
