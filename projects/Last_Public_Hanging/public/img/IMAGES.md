# Last Public Hanging - archival images

All files below were downloaded with `tools/find_images.py` / `tools/commons.py`, or cut from Library of Congress Chronicling America newspaper scans (IIIF full-resolution page images). Every file has a credit in `credits.json`. Sizes are pixels (w x h). Newspaper crops were converted to greyscale. The two Sanborn sheets and the Ames factory panorama are in colour or sepia. Desaturate them in the edit to match the channel's black-and-white look.

**Rights summary.**
- **Newspapers (Chronicling America):** LOC says "The Library of Congress believes that the newspapers in Chronicling America are in the public domain or have no known copyright restrictions." All are US publications from 1936 or 1938, so they are public domain unless the copyright was renewed.
- **Renewal checks:**
  - The Online Books Page periodical-renewal survey lists *Atlanta Daily World* and *Brownsville Herald* as "no issue renewals, no contribution renewals".
  - I searched the 1963 and 1964 *Catalog of Copyright Entries* renewals for Works of Art and Photographs. That is where 1936 photographs would have to be renewed. No AP, INS or NEA renewal for the Owensboro photos turned up. NEA renewed only feature series, such as the Dionne quintuplets pictures.
  - The *Evening Star* photos carry a printed "Copyright, A. P. Wirephoto" line. No registration or renewal was found, so they are treated as PD.
- **Other sources:**
  - LOC FSA and Sing Sing photos are marked "No known restrictions".
  - The Commons files are PD, except `florence_thompson_portrait.jpg`, which is **CC BY-SA 3.0**. It needs the on-screen or description credit "Daviess County Sheriff's Office / CC BY-SA 3.0".

**Strongest picks for the Short (vertical-friendly):** `adw_1936-08-16_man_hangs_20000_jeer_scaffold.jpg` (vertical, scaffold before the drop), `crowd_owensboro_1936_left_vertical_ivp.jpg`, `sunday_star_1936-08-16_they_are_watching_a_man_die.jpg`, `florence_thompson_1936_woman_sheriff_to_be_hangman.jpg`, `san_antonio_light_1936-08-14_banner_crop.jpg`, `charles_w_anderson_jr_1938_adw.jpg`.
