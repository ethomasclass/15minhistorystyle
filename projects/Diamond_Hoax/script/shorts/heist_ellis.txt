Before Theranos. Before Fyre Festival. Two cousins sold America's richest men a field of dirt, for about eighteen million dollars in today's money. Here's how.

Step one: buy the bait. Cheap, flawed stones nobody wanted.

Step two: play hard to get. Show up at night with the bag. Refuse to say where it's from.

Step three: borrow someone's trust. Get Charles Tiffany to look at them.

Step four: make a mine. Push the stones into an empty Colorado mesa. Even into anthills.

Step five: get out in time. Let the experts dig them up, take the money, and leave.

[pause] It worked. Until a government geologist found the field. It was too perfect.

A dozen rubies by every diamond. And rubies only in anthills with boot prints around them. [dry] Nature is not that tidy.

They hadn't sold a diamond mine. They'd sold dirt.
