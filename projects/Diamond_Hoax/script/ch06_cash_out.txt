Now the real money moved. The new company had more than a quarter of a billion dollars in stock. The names on the list were huge. One of those generals. The Rothschild banking family. Tiffany himself.

And it spread. Newspapers printed how-to articles on diamond mining. Prospectors fanned out across the West, hunting for diamond fields of their own. People called it *diamond fever*.

That's move five: *the rush*. Once everybody important is buying in, nobody wants to be the one asking questions. They just want in before it's gone.

[pause] And that's exactly when a smart con man leaves. Move six: *the exit*. Most crooks get greedy and stay one round too long. The cousins didn't. The investors bought them out completely: about 15 million dollars for Arnold, nearly three million for Slack. Arnold went home to Kentucky, a rich man. Slack just disappeared.

[light] For a few months, it looked like they'd gotten away with it. Then, in California, a geologist got on the wrong train. Or the right one.
