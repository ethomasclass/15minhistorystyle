# Suno prompts: They Fooled America's Richest Men (the Great Diamond Hoax)

Copy each **Style** box (and its **Lyrics** tags box) into Suno. Everything else on this page is labels. File names start with
`dh_` (Diamond Hoax). Each cue is written for this video but also for reuse: any con, swindle, bubble, heist or fraud story from the
1850s to the 1900s.

**The sound of this video:** 1870s parlor instruments with a sly grin. Upright parlor piano, pizzicato strings, a muted cornet,
low clarinet and bassoon, tuba, a little snare brush and wood block. Smug and playful on top, a thread of menace underneath. It
turns serious in the last third (the race, the dominoes, Ralston) and comes back to the theme, quieter and wiser, for the ending.

**Tempos:** the narration runs about 165 words a minute, so the cues sit at a relaxed 76–100 BPM except the race.

## Settings (same for every cue)

- **Custom** mode, **Instrumental OFF**, and paste only the tags box into Lyrics (every line is in [brackets] and it starts with
  [Instrumental], so there are no words to sing). If a take hums or sings, throw it out.
- **Style:** paste the cue's box.
- **Exclude styles** (every cue except `dh_diamond_fever`, which has its own):

```
vocals, choir, lyrics, humming, drum kit, EDM, trap, pop, rock, synth lead, dubstep, rap, gospel vocals, whistling, western movie theme, harmonica, electric guitar
```

- Make **2–4 takes**; keep the one whose **first 30–40 seconds** already sound right.
- **Length:** the minimums below come from the voiced chapters (plus a little room). Longer is always fine: I trim and fade. If a
  take is short, **Extend** from its last good section.
- **Download as MP3**, name it exactly as shown, and send it back. I'll measure, level and place each one.
