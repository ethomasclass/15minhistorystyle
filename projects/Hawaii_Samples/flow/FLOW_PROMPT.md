# Google Flow prompt: Hawaiʻi portal journey (retro faceted-poster style)

Nine images: six scene plates and three cut-out sheets. Upload `flow/style_reference.jpg` with the prompt as the style
reference. Put the results in `flow/in/` with the file names given (or just send them over and say which is which).

Two special colours do real work, so they matter more than anything else in the prompt:
- **Magenta `#FF00FF`** marks a doorway (porthole glass, picture inside a frame, window opening, sheet of paper). The
  camera flies through it and the next scene is put behind it.
- **Green `#00FF00`** is the background of the cut-out sheets, so figures and props can be lifted off.

## Master prompt (paste whole)

```
You are illustrating a history documentary about the overthrow of the Hawaiian Kingdom in 1893. Create 9 separate images, one per numbered item below. Make each one its own image in 16:9 landscape format at the highest resolution available. Do not combine items, do not make a grid or collage, and do not number or caption the images. Use the attached image as the style reference for all nine.

STYLE FOR EVERY IMAGE (identical across all 9 so they look like one series):
A mid-century retro travel-poster illustration made of flat, faceted shapes. Every shape is one flat colour with crisp, slightly angular edges; forms are built from a few flat planes, lit from the upper left, with a lighter plane on the lit side and a darker plane on the shade side. No outlines, no line work, no gradients, no airbrushing, no painterly brushstrokes, no photographic detail, no 3D render look. Skies are flat dusty blue with large flat cream clouds that have one darker cream band along the bottom. Mountains and rocks are faceted like folded paper. Trees and palms are angular shards of two or three greens on thin dark trunks. A faint aged-paper grain over everything.

PALETTE (use these and close neighbours only):
paper cream #E9DBB8 and #F2E6C6; dusty sky blue #A7B9B6; sea teal-grey #6F9997 and #4F7B7D; olive and moss greens #A3A45A, #6C733C, #3F4527; warm stone #CFC4AB and #A79C86; slate roof grey #666D74 and #3F454C; koa and walnut wood #8A5A35 and #5A4130; deep room green #2A3A33; near-black plum #2B2630; ship white #ECE4D0; brick red #8F3B2E; ink #3A3934. One small spot colour per image, chosen from burnt orange #D4732C, dusty pink #D98B8C or mustard #DFB43A.
Do NOT use bright turquoise or bright pure orange anywhere: those are reserved for graphics added later.

PEOPLE:
Flat simple figures in the same faceted style, with correct period clothing. Faces are a plain flat skin-tone shape with NO eyes, nose, mouth or other features. No recognizable real person: the Queen is always seen from behind or three-quarter back, face not visible. Native Hawaiian people have brown skin and are drawn with dignity, never caricatured.

RULES FOR EVERY IMAGE:
- Historically accurate for Honolulu in January 1893: clothing, uniforms, ships, buildings, furniture, lamps, flags. Nothing modern.
- No text of any kind: no letters, words, numbers, captions, signatures, labels or watermarks anywhere, including on flags, papers, charts and the ship.
- No frame, border or margin. Fill the whole 16:9 image.
- No gore, no violence.

DOORWAYS (items 2, 3, 5 and 6): each of these images contains exactly one "doorway" shape. Fill that shape with completely flat pure magenta, hex #FF00FF: no shading, no reflections, no glass highlights, no texture, no objects, bars or people overlapping it. Draw it facing the viewer straight on, with no perspective distortion. Keep it away from the edges of the image. Do not use magenta anywhere else.

CUT-OUT SHEETS (items 7, 8 and 9): place everything on a completely flat pure green background, hex #00FF00, with no floor, no cast shadows and no gradients. Keep clear green space between the separate figures or objects; nothing overlaps or touches, and nothing is cut off by the edge. Do not use that green anywhere inside the figures or objects.

THE 9 IMAGES:

1. p1_harbour — Honolulu Harbor, January 1893, a wide view from the shore. Calm sea-teal water. At anchor, seen exactly side-on and filling the middle of the picture, the U.S. Navy protected cruiser USS Boston of the 1880s: a long low white hull with a brick-red waterline stripe, a row of small round portholes along the hull, a buff deckhouse, one buff funnel with a black top, two masts with yards and rigging, a gun behind a shield at the bow and at the stern, ship's boats on davits, a small United States flag at the stern. Behind, the faceted profile of Diamond Head across the water and a low green shore. Two angular coconut palms frame the left edge. Bright morning. No doorway in this image.

2. p2_hull_porthole — A close view of the side of the same white ship's hull, seen straight on, filling the whole image: flat white hull plating with a few faint plate seams, the brick-red waterline stripe along the bottom edge and a strip of sea below it. In the exact centre, one round porthole with a thick brass rim and six small brass bolts. DOORWAY: the round glass of the porthole, a perfect circle whose diameter is 40 percent of the image height, flat magenta.

3. p3_wardroom — The officers' wardroom inside the ship, seen straight on like a stage set. A wall of warm wood panelling under dark deck beams; a brass oil lamp hanging from a beam, glowing softly; two brass portholes on the left of the wall showing sky and sea; a long wooden table across the lower part of the picture with a nautical chart of an island (no lettering), a pair of brass dividers, a coffee cup and a naval officer's cap; a small brass wall clock with no numerals. No people. On the right half of the wall hangs a picture in a wide gilt frame. DOORWAY: the picture inside the gilt frame, a 16:9 rectangle about 30 percent of the image width, flat magenta.

4. p4_palace — ʻIolani Palace, Honolulu, 1893, the front seen straight on and symmetrical. A pale stone palace on a raised basement: two floors of deep arcaded verandas with slender columns and balustrades, a cornice with a balustrade along the roofline, a square tower at each corner and a taller square tower in the centre over the arched entrance, each tower topped by a slate-grey mansard roof with an iron cresting. A wide stone staircase leads to the entrance. On a tall pole on the central tower flies the flag of the Hawaiian Kingdom: eight horizontal stripes of white, red and blue with the British Union flag in the upper corner by the pole. Lawn and a straight path in front, coconut palms at both sides, faceted green mountains and flat cream clouds behind. No doorway in this image.

5. p5_tower_window — A close view of the upper storey of the palace's central tower, seen straight on: pale stone wall with corner quoins, a moulded cornice above, part of the slate mansard roof at the top edge and the roofline balustrade at the bottom edge. In the wall, three tall round-arched windows side by side with stone surrounds; the left and right windows are dark glass. DOORWAY: the middle window's whole opening (tall, with a round arched top), its height about 45 percent of the image height, flat magenta, with no glazing bars across it.

6. p6_queens_room — A private room in the palace, January 1893, seen straight on like a stage set, with the desk drawn in flat poster perspective as if seen from slightly above. Deep green walls with a wooden picture rail and dark wood wainscot; a tall window with pale curtains on the left, garden light coming through; on the right a pair of kāhili (Hawaiian royal feather standards: tall poles topped with cylinders of red and yellow feathers); a large koa-wood desk across the lower centre with a brass oil lamp with a green shade, an inkwell and a dip pen, and a neat stack of papers. No people. DOORWAY: one single sheet of paper lying on the desk, drawn as a plain 16:9 rectangle with no perspective foreshortening, about 22 percent of the image width, tilted no more than 10 degrees, flat magenta, with nothing lying on it.

7. c1_queen — CUT-OUT SHEET on flat green. The same woman four times, full body, evenly spaced in a row, all the same height: a Native Hawaiian queen in her fifties in 1893, dark hair pinned up, a long dark plum silk gown with a high neck and long sleeves, a cream sash from the right shoulder to the left hip with a small star-shaped order on the chest. In every pose she is seen from behind or three-quarter back, face not visible. Pose 1: standing, leaning slightly forward with one hand resting on a table edge (the table is not drawn), as if signing a paper. Pose 2: standing upright, hands clasped in front. Pose 3: standing with one hand raised to hold back a curtain (the curtain is not drawn), looking out. Pose 4: seated on a simple upholstered chair, turned away.

8. c2_navy — CUT-OUT SHEET on flat green. Five separate full-body figures, evenly spaced, all to the same scale, with featureless faces. Figure 1: a U.S. Navy officer of 1893 in a dark blue frock coat with two rows of brass buttons, white trousers and a peaked cap, seated in profile on a plain chair, leaning forward and writing (the table is not drawn). Figure 2: the same officer standing, looking through a brass spyglass held to his eye. Figures 3, 4 and 5: three U.S. Navy sailors of the landing force in dark blue jumpers and trousers, flat blue caps, tan canvas leggings and cartridge belts, each standing at attention with a rifle held upright at the right shoulder, each slightly different in height and build.

9. c3_props — CUT-OUT SHEET on flat green. Seven separate objects, evenly spaced, each seen straight on: (a) a pair of kāhili, tall poles with red and yellow feather cylinders, on small wooden stands; (b) a hanging brass oil lamp with a glass chimney; (c) a glass inkwell with a dip pen resting in it; (d) a rolled nautical chart tied with a ribbon; (e) three rifles stacked together in a tripod; (f) the flag of the Hawaiian Kingdom drawn flat as a rectangle: eight horizontal stripes of white, red and blue with the British Union flag in the upper left corner; (g) the United States flag of 1893 drawn flat as a rectangle: thirteen red and white stripes and a blue canton with small white stars. No lettering on anything.
```

## Fallback: one prompt per image

one at a time, and still attach the style reference:

```

Muted palette of paper cream, dusty sky blue, sea teal-grey, olive greens, warm stone, slate grey, koa wood browns, deep room green, plum black, ship white and brick red, with one small spot of burnt orange, dusty pink or mustard; no bright turquoise and no bright pure orange. ## What to check before sending them back
- The magenta is one flat colour with a clean edge, and nothing crosses it.
- The doorway is not squashed by perspective (a circle is a circle, the page is a rectangle).
- No lettering crept in (charts, the ship's stern, the flags).
- Figures on the green sheets do not touch each other or the edge.
- All nine look like one series. If one drifts, regenerate just that one with the fallback style line.
