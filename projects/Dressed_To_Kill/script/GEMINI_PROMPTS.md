# Dressed to Kill: Gemini painting prompts (silhouette style)

**Style chosen:** Victorian cut-paper silhouettes on cream, with arsenic green as the only colour (your pick over the oil paintings
and the graphic novel; tests in `public/img/gen/tests/`). Eight moments, four must-haves. Everything else in the video is archival.

**How to run them**
- Gemini / Flow image generation, largest size, aspect **16:9**. Paste the **whole** prompt including the style paragraph (identical in all eight).
- Regenerate rather than fix: any text, letters or signs; modern objects; a mint or blue-green green; a face with recognisable features.
- Upload the PNGs with these file names. Any scene still works without its painting.
- On screen: shown as they are (cream, black, green), tagged `Illustration · <what it shows>`. If a green comes out minty, I shift the hue in post.
