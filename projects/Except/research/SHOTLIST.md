# Except: archival shot list

Save to `public/img/chNN/` with descriptive names. Get an alternate for each must-have.

## ch01 Cold Open
- MUST: The 13th Amendment (enrolled joint resolution, National Archives scan; or one of the signed commemorative copies at LoC)
- A close crop on the "except as a punishment for crime" line, so the scan must be high resolution
- Lee portrait (1863) for "Lee's army was marching into Pennsylvania"

## ch02 One Week in July
- MUST: Gettysburg battle lithograph or engraving (Pickett's Charge; e.g., Edwin Forbes sketch, or a Currier & Ives print)
- Gettysburg town view 1863 (photo)
- Gettysburg landscape (Little Round Top, Cemetery Ridge) photos; avoid graphic death photos
- George Meade portrait
- MUST: Vicksburg siege (engraving or lithograph; Union lines, caves in Vicksburg)
- MUST: Ulysses S. Grant portrait (1863–64)
- Grant receiving lieutenant general commission, March 1864 (engraving)

## ch03 A New Birth of Freedom
- MUST: The Gettysburg Address manuscript (one of the five; LoC has the Nicolay and Hay copies)
- MUST: The only known photo of Lincoln at Gettysburg (crowd photo, Nov 19, 1863)
- Edward Everett portrait
- Soldiers' National Cemetery, Gettysburg
- Declaration of Independence (for "1776")

## ch04 Who Carried the War? (quiet)
- MUST: New York draft riots engravings: the draft office (Third Avenue) and the burning of the Colored Orphan Asylum (Harper's / Illustrated London News). NO caricatured rioters.
- The draft lottery (wheel) engraving
- MUST: Draft/commutation: a $300 receipt or "substitute broker" ad, or Enrollment Act broadside
- Confederate conscription broadside
- Richmond bread riot engraving (Frank Leslie's, May 1863)
- Confederate currency (inflation), a Confederate bill

## ch05 Hard War
- MUST: William T. Sherman portrait
- MUST: Atlanta 1864 (George N. Barnard photos: the car shed, destroyed railroad, Union soldiers in Atlanta)
- Sherman's March: "Sherman's march to the sea" engraving; destroyed railroad (Barnard, "Sherman's men destroying railroad")
- Savannah 1864–65
- McClellan 1864 campaign poster (Lincoln vs. McClellan)
- Lincoln 1864 campaign poster

## ch06 Appomattox
- MUST: McLean House, Appomattox (1865 photo)
- Surrender at Appomattox print (any period lithograph)
- Lee after surrender (Brady, April 1865)
- MUST: Ford's Theatre (photo) and the "assassination" print (Currier & Ives; not graphic)
- Lincoln funeral or mourning broadside
- MUST: General Order No. 3 (Juneteenth) document, or Galveston 1865 image
- Emancipation celebration, Texas (1900 Juneteenth celebration photo, Austin, exists at the Austin History Center; LoC may have "Emancipation Day" photos)

## ch07 Except (quiet)
- MUST: House chamber celebrating passage, January 31, 1865 (Harper's Weekly engraving, "Scene in the House")
- MUST: The 13th Amendment again (close crop)
- Georgia capitol at Milledgeville (ratification)
- MUST: Black Codes document: Mississippi 1865 laws (printed text) or a vagrancy notice
- Convict leasing photo (pre-1929; e.g., Georgia convicts in a camp, 1880s–1900s, LoC has some)

## ch08 What It Settled
- Reuse; plus a Reconstruction-era image for the final beat (freedpeople's school, 1866; or "The First Vote," Harper's 1867)
