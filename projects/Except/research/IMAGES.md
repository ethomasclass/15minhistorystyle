# Except: archival images

All files are in `public/img/chNN/`, and every one has a credit in `public/img/credits.json`. Contact sheets: `out/contact_ch01.jpg` to `out/contact_ch08.jpg`.
Sizes are in pixels. Sources: `loc:` = Library of Congress, `iabook:` = a page from a pre-1930 book on the Internet Archive, `ia:` = an Internet Archive item, `commons` = Wikimedia Commons.

**Note:** the ch01 to ch06 folders also hold files that another task added (Stephens *Constitutional View* pages, Davis *Rise and Fall*, 1860 Lincoln campaign items, Montgomery state house, Secession Hall, slave population maps, Scott's Great Snake). They don't belong to this shot list. I left them alone, and they are not on the contact sheets.

## Gaps

Not found as usable public domain archival files (possible AI images or drawn graphics):

- **ch03 MUST, real manuscript scan:** the Gettysburg Address is covered by 1909 book facsimiles (Bliss and a second copy), not by a photo of an original. The LoC Nicolay and Hay scans (mal4356500, mal4356600) have no rights field, so the tool refused them. Commons `File:Blisscopy-1.jpg` (2211x2746) and `File:Gettysburg Address Bliss copy.jpg` were blocked by HTTP 429 every time. Retry them later if you want a scan of the real paper.
- **ch04 MUST, $300 commutation receipt / Enrollment Act broadside:** none found. The bounty-broker photo and the "Wanted a Substitute" sheet music cover the beat.
- **ch04 Third Avenue draft office:** no 1863 image in the API. The best stand-in is the 1873 Headley frontispiece. The LoC item "mob burning the provost Marshal's office" (2022637529) returned 404.
- **ch04 Confederate conscription broadside:** none. "Freemen! Avoid conscription!" (2022651888) returned 404, and it may be a Union item anyway.
- **ch04 Richmond bread riot (Leslie's, May 1863):** the only period image found ("Sowing and Reaping", Leslie's May 23, 1863) includes a racist caricature, so I rejected it. Gap.
- **ch04 Confederate currency:** only 1875 photographic plates. The LoC single-note items had no rights statement.
- **ch05 Savannah 1864–65 city view:** only Waud's sketch of Sherman's review in Savannah. No city photo.
- **ch06 MUST, General Order No. 3, page 1:** I have only page 2 (the end of Order No. 3). The same IA item has `general-order-3-juneteenth page 1.jpg`, but the tool takes only the largest file. A Galveston 1865 view: none.
- **ch06 Texas Juneteenth celebration (Austin, 1900):** not at LoC through this tool. The 1866 DC emancipation celebration stands in.
- **ch07 Milledgeville:** only a 1930s HABS photo of the old capitol. No period image.
- **ch07 1865 original printing of the Mississippi code:** only the 1906 reprint. The 1865 called-session book on IA is the Feb–Mar 1865 Confederate session, not the November code.
- **ch02 Grant receiving his commission (engraving):** not found. Two 1864 "Lieut. Gen. Grant" portraits stand in.

- **Later images:** Darley's march (c.1883), the Declaration facsimile (1903), the Headley frontispiece (1873), the currency plates (1875), Fleming (1906), the Meade Hicks painting (a reproduction), and the HABS Milledgeville photo (1930s). Label them as later.
- **Uncertain:** the second Gettysburg Address facsimile is labelled "Hay copy" from its wording ("upon this continent", corrections between the lines). Check Lambert's caption on page 21 before you name it on screen.
- **Headley frontispiece:** it is printed sideways in the book, so rotate it.
