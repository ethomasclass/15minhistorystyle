## Gaps

Shot-list items with no satisfactory archival image (candidates for AI engravings or drawn graphics):

- **ch01 Union blockading fleet off Charleston (engraving).** Only Volck's 1864 stone-fleet etching (Confederate-sympathizer artist, one wreck) and the Leslie's harbor plan. LoC "Sealing up Charleston Harbor" (loc:2004667759) and "The blockade of Charleston" (loc:00652823) failed in the tool (no file / no rights statement). Draw it or make an AI engraving.
- **ch03 Gunpowder River bridge specifically.** Covered by the Harper's May 11 1861 cut of the mob burning the Canton bridge (Baltimore) and a generic 1862 Virginia bridge ruin. No period image of the Gunpowder or Bush River bridges was found.
- **ch03 Ex parte Merryman document.** Not found; Fort McHenry (where Merryman was held) is covered twice.
- **ch04 New York Tribune "Prayer of Twenty Millions" page (Aug 20 1862).** Not found. Substitute: Lincoln's reply typeset in a 1908 booklet (two pages). Ideally the Tribune or National Intelligencer page should be sourced elsewhere (Chronicling America/LoC newspapers was not reachable with this tool).
- **ch04 a women's field/farm work image.** Only Homer's "Our Women and the War" spread (nursing, sewing, letter writing) and a Sanitary Commission lodge (men only). No women farming or spying.
- **ch05 MUST map of areas covered by the Proclamation.** No period coverage map found. The 1861 Hergesheimer slave-population map is in ch05 as a base (it is only 1920 px from Commons, CC BY 2.0). Plan: draw the coverage on the 3D map as the script notes.
- **ch05 Watch Night / Nast "Emancipation" (Harper's Jan 24 1863).** LoC copy refused (Goldstein collection rights restricted). Leslie's "Emancipation Day in South Carolina" and the freed-people-entering-lines cuts are the stand-ins.
- **ch06 cotton in Egypt or India (period engraving).** Not found. Stand-in: cotton bales at Savannah, 1865.
- **ch06 British Parliament (period engraving).** Not searched beyond LoC (nothing). Palmerston and Gladstone portraits cover the "British leaders" line.
- **ch06 Punch cartoon, 1862-63.** LoC "Cotton in the stocks" (loc:2022886759) and another 1862 British cartoon returned 404 from the tool. Not downloaded; no gap in the narration if skipped.
- **ch07 Lewis Douglass in uniform.** The only copy found shows him in civilian dress c1870 with his wife (Commons, 555 to 1594 px). A uniform photo exists (Moorland-Spingarn / NMAAHC) but was not reachable.
