# YouTube text: The Pledge's Forgotten Salute (Short)

Title: The Pledge of Allegiance Used to Have a Different Salute
Alt: Why American Kids Saluted Like This Until 1942

Description:
For 50 years, American schoolchildren said the Pledge of Allegiance with a straight-arm salute. It started on Columbus Day in 1892, and Congress changed it to hand over heart in December 1942. Here's why.

Photos: Southington, Connecticut, May 1942, Fenno Jacobs, Office of War Information (Library of Congress); schoolchildren saluting the flag, Hampton, Virginia, 1899, Frances Benjamin Johnston (Library of Congress); The Youth's Companion, Sept. 8, 1892 (Internet Archive).
