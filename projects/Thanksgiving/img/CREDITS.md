## Rights notes

**Item 9: Star-Kist "Monterey Soufflé Salad" ad, *Woman's Day*, June 1955. DO NOT USE (not downloaded).**
- The issue **was renewed**: US Copyright Office record **RE0000156474**, "Woman's day. Vol. 18, no. 9, June 1955",
  renewed 7 Jan 1983 by CBS Publications (search: publicrecords.copyright.gov, title "Woman's day 1955").
  The whole 1955 run was renewed, so the magazine is under copyright until the end of 2050.
- No renewal by Star-Kist turns up (searches for "Star-Kist", "Star Kist", "Starkist", "Monterey souffle salad").
  Star-Kist did register some of its own ads as commercial prints (for example KK179068, *Life*, 1964), so it may have
  registered this one too. A contributor's ad is normally not covered by the publisher's renewal, so the ad is
  *probably* public domain. That rests on a legal argument, though, not a clear record, and the only scan we know of
  belongs to a commercial site (clickamericana.com), which is not an allowed source.
- **Recommendation:** don't show it. Keep the line in the script and show
  `knox-1915-perfection-salad-and-tuna-fish-salad-p26.jpg` (a 1915 tuna salad set in gelatine, public domain) or
  `usda-family-fare-jellied-meat-fish-salad-p64.jpg` (a USDA mid-century jellied fish salad, a federal work).
  If the ad must appear, get a lawyer's sign-off and our own scan of an original issue first.

**Other cautions**
- `knox-dainty-desserts-1915-cover.jpg` includes a racist caricature of a Black child. Crop to the jelly and the title, or
  use the title page instead.
- Trademarks: Knox, Jell-O, Angelus, Cracker Jack and Piggly Wiggly marks on PD items are fine as history; don't imply endorsement.
  No Ocean Spray material was used. Its logo is still a live trademark, and no PD Ocean Spray item was found.
- `simmons-1796-to-dress-a-turtle-p1.jpg` (from the earlier search) is **page 19 and has no turtle on it**. The turtle
  recipe starts at the foot of `..._p2.jpg` (p.20), goes on through `_p21` and ends on
  `simmons-1796-calves-head-turtle-fashion-p22.jpg` (Madeira, then the Calve's Head). `src/ch/ch34/parts.tsx` and
  `src/kit/tg_endboard.tsx` still point at `p1`. The Simmons scan is the LOC 1796 Hartford first edition (it does include
  the turtle and the Calve's Head), mirrored on IA at a higher resolution. The IA item `simmons-american-cookery` is a
  modern retypeset edition with unclear provenance and was NOT used.
- RESEARCH.md says the 1870 Everett House menu has no oyster soup. The original scan lists **"SOUP. Clam. Oysters. Hunter's."**,
  plus raw, fried and escalloped oysters.
