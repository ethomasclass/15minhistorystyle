In 1796, an American cookbook told you how to start dinner. "About nine o'clock, hang up your Turtle by the hind fins, cut off the head and save the blood."

That's step one.

Turtle soup was one of the fanciest dishes in America. Green sea turtles, shipped up alive from the Caribbean. If turtle was on your table, you had money.

So people who didn't... faked it. The same cookbook: take a calf's head. Open it up. Take out the brains. Cook it until it tastes, more or less, like turtle. Mock turtle.

The real turtles were hunted so hard, their numbers fell by about ninety-seven percent. Turtle soup disappeared.

The fake outlasted the real thing.
