So, why did Buddhism vanish from its birthplace?

Because of how it spread. Hinduism, an ethnic religion, stayed rooted in India and traveled mainly when Indians traveled. Sikhism welcomed everyone but also spread with migrating families. Buddhism was different: it was carried by an emperor and by traders to new places that made it their own. Once it lived everywhere, it didn't need its hearth anymore.

Which is why, 2,500 years later, the people sitting under this tree mostly come from somewhere else.
