# When Europe Ate Mummies - archival images

All files are public domain, CC0, CC BY or (flagged) CC BY-SA. Credits for every file are in `credits.json` (title, artist, date, licence, page).
Sources: Internet Archive scans (pre-1930 books: Pomet 1694/1712/1725, Quincy 1718, Pettigrew 1834, Field 1835, Twain 1869, Munsell 1876, Weber 1888, Merck 1903, Braun & Hogenberg 1572), Wellcome Collection (PDM / CC BY), Library of Congress (no known restrictions), Art Institute of Chicago / Cleveland Museum / Met (CC0), Wikimedia Commons (PD / CC BY / CC BY-SA), NYPL scans of the Description de l'Egypte (PD).
Colour items are museum photos or hand-coloured prints: desaturate for the channel's black-and-white look. Sizes are pixels (w x h).

**Licence notes:** CC BY items need an on-screen/description credit (Wellcome Collection, CC BY 4.0; Zinnmann for the Apotheken-Museum jar). Two files are CC BY-SA 3.0 (marked `_ccbysa`): credit 'Bullenwachter, CC BY-SA 3.0'. Tut photos are LOC items published c.1926 with 'no known restrictions' (US public domain: published before 1931). No AI images; no close-up photographs of human remains (Pettigrew's coloured head/body plates were deliberately skipped).
