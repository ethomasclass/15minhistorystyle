## 4 · A real recipe  (~51 s)
**Cover:** "THIS WAS A REAL RECIPE." over the index card, with "red-haired (required)" circled.
**Title:** `A Real 1609 Recipe for Making Your Own Mummy`

1. (ch5) *Take the body of a red-haired man. Twenty-four years old. Hanged, or broken on the wheel.* (the card writes itself; read flat)
2. (ch5) *That's a real recipe. It was printed in a medical book, and other doctors copied it for decades.* (cut to Croll's printed Latin page, "ruffi" circled)
3. (ch5) *A popular theory said… young and healthy who died suddenly… a fresh body beat a three-thousand-year-old pharaoh.* (the mummy case crossed out, the FRESH jar fills)
4. (ch5) *So in 1609, a German chemist named Oswald Croll published a recipe for making your own.* (Croll's title page, stamped 1609)
5. (ch5) *Leave him out for a day and a night… myrrh, aloe and spirit of wine. Dry.* (back to the card, the remaining boxes tick)
6. (ch5) *Why red hair?… thinner, and his flesh better. Which, honestly, is the least disturbing part of that sentence.* (the 1669 English page, "red-hair'd" circled) **PAYOFF**
