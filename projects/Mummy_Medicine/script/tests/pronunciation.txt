Mumia vera aegyptiaca. Genuine Egyptian mummy, from Merck.

In Persian and Arabic, mumiya was a mineral. Gerard of Cremona translated it. Paracelsus praised it.

Guy de la Fontaine visited Alexandria. Pierre Pomet complained about Venice and Lyon.

Oswald Croll wrote the recipe. Michel de Montaigne asked who the cannibals were.
