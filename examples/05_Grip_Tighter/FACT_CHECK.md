# Fact check: Grip Tighter

Every date, number, quote, name and causal claim in the narration was checked against primary sources and published scholarship, chapter by chapter. Verdicts:
- **✓** correct as written.
- **≈** correct, with nuance worth knowing.
- **✎** reworded after this check.
- **✗** wrong (none were left in the narration).

**Changes made because of this check:** ch01 death toll; ch02 Jefferson/Madison numbers and the 1808 wording; ch03 "gin" coinage, Indian Removal Act wording, "inland South"; ch04 Harriett Hill quote; ch05 political power, plantation mistresses, "slave states," "for many"; ch06 babies in the field, "two families to a cabin"; ch07 *State v. Mann* year, overseers, Celia's defense; ch08 rebellion plots; ch09 executions; ch10 the 73–58 vote, anti-literacy timing, "most valuable export."

**Errors found outside the narration:**
- SCRIPT.md's source for Harriett Hill said the Georgia volume. Her interview is in **Vol. II, Arkansas, Part 3** (interviewed in Forrest City, Ark.; born in Lithonia, Ga.).
- The lecture's title-slide portrait is Copley's *Head of a Negro* (1777–78), not the Exeter painting I first named.
- The eclipse was annular, but Southampton was **just outside** the ring's path: about 96% of the sun covered, so no ring was visible there.

**Limits:**
- Encyclopedia Virginia, loc.gov, Monticello and docsouth.unc.edu blocked automated reading.
- Their claims were checked through search excerpts or identical texts on Project Gutenberg (Gray's *Confessions* #15333, Douglass #23, Jacobs #11030, Northup #45631, the FWP narratives).
- Spot-check those rows in a browser before publishing.
