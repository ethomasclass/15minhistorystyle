## Description

In December 1913, 71-year-old Ambrose Bierce rode with Pancho Villa's army, wrote that he was leaving "for an unknown destination," and was never seen again. How does one man live that many lives?

Before he vanished in the Mexican Revolution, Ambrose Bierce was a Civil War soldier shot in the head at Kennesaw Mountain, a Union mapmaker at Shiloh, a humor writer in London, a gold-mine manager in Deadwood, the most feared newspaper columnist in America, the author of The Devil's Dictionary and "An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge," and the reporter who took on railroad tycoon Collis Huntington and won. In 15 minutes or less, we follow all seven lives, from a log cabin in Ohio to a desert in Chihuahua, and look at the theories about what happened to him: Ojinaga, a firing squad at Sierra Mojada, the Grand Canyon, and yes, the aliens.
