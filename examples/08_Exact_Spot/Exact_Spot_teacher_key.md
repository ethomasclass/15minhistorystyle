# The Exact Spot: Teacher Key

Answers use the narration's own words. Times match the student guide.

**Open with (0:31).** Rio Grande; Nueces.

## Part 1 · The Texas Revolution

1. "Where is the spot?"
2. **True.**
3. **False.** "Texas talks its way into an exception."
4. Centralism: "the national government decides, not the states." General Antonio López de Santa Anna.
5. "Both, at once." To the settlers it was "a fight against a dictator for rights they'd been promised," but their constitution protected slavery: "Freedom for some Texans. Slavery for others." (Also accept: the Declaration doesn't mention slavery, "not once.")
6. Santa Anna.
7. **True.**

## Part 2 · Polk acts first

8. "The belief that the United States was meant, even chosen by God, to spread across the whole continent."
9. The Rio Grande. Mexican cavalry surround the patrol; more than a dozen Americans are killed or wounded, and most of the rest are captured.
10. "Congress declared it. But the President put the army where a fight was likely, and Congress voted after the shooting started."

## Part 3 · Americans disagree

11. "unjust"
12. **False.** "Some blame Mexico. Others blame Polk."

## Part 4 · The treaty and the cost

13. The Rio Grande; about 530,000 square miles; 15 million dollars.
14. Article 10 (it protected their land grants). Congress made them "prove their titles in court."
15. About 1,700; more than 11,500.
16. Any two: Native nations ("No one asked them"); enslaved people in Texas ("No one asked them either"); the about 80,000 Mexicans in the Cession ("They didn't move. The border did."); California landowners who lost land; Juan Seguín.

## Part 5 · New land reopens slavery

17. "slavery"
18. **False.** It passed the House, "dies in the Senate," and "never becomes law."
19. "Loyalty to your region over your party."
20. "Texas was already a slave state when it joined. This land was new. Every acre forced the question: slave, or free?"
