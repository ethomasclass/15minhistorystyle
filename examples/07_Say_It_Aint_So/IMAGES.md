## Gaps and caveats

- **Not found (PD):** William Wansley / the 1865 Mutuals-Eckford game itself; any Brooklyn Eckfords image; Al Nichols, Bill Craver; Dick Higham; the Giants' team doctor (1908 bribe); a jury photo from the 1921 trial; Landis on the bench in a courtroom; a 1920s clubhouse interior; a 19th-century engraving that specifically shows *pool selling* (closest: McCabe 1882 gambling-hall engravings plus the text page describing pool-selling).
- **Jim Devlin:** only a small (232x397) Louisville-era image exists. The larger "Jim Devlin" Old Judge cards on Commons/Met are a *different* Jim Devlin (St. Louis Browns, 1887-88) and were not used.
- **"Pool room" photos (ch04):** the two Hine photos (1910, 1912) are billiard halls. Use them for atmosphere only, not as a betting parlour.
- **Rothstein:** the two LOC portraits (NY World-Telegram collection) are marked "publication may be restricted" and were skipped. The Commons copies used were published 1915-1921.
- **Post-1925 items:** `ch08/landis_1937.jpg` (Harris & Ewing, LOC says "no known restrictions on publication") and the HABS courthouse photos (1964, US government work). Both are flagged here for review.
- **1919 newsreel:** `ch06/pathe_ws1919_*` are stills from *1919 Pathe World Series* (Commons, public domain, 4:30 long, 1080p transcode). The video itself is also usable for motion. Frames are cropped to the 4:3 picture area (1440x1072) and are soft (film-era resolution).
- **Panoramas** (`ch04/*panorama*`, `shibe_park_world_series_c1910.jpg`) were re-saved from LOC TIFFs at up to 9000 px wide for slow pans.
- `ch01/landis_street_1924_crop.jpg` was made by the main session from `landis_national_photo_1924.jpg`, so it shares that file's credit.
