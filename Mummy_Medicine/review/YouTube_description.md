## Description
```
Why did Europeans eat mummies as medicine, and how was "genuine Egyptian mummy" still for sale in a drug catalog in 1924? In 1903, the German drug company Merck listed it by the kilo, right under the musk: "Mumia vera aegyptiaca... while supplies last."

This is the history of mummy medicine in under 10 minutes: a translation mistake that turned a mineral called mumiya into the mummy, the tomb trade from Cairo to Europe's apothecaries, fake mummies made from recent bodies, a 1609 recipe for making your own, medicinal cannibalism, Victorian mummy unwrapping parties, and the paint called mummy brown.
```
